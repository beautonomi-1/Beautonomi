# Information Security Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets Beautonomi's commitment to protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of the information we handle for customers, providers and staff, and to meeting our legal obligations, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

## 2. Scope

Everyone who works for or with Beautonomi — employees, contractors and suppliers with access — and all systems in the ISMS scope.

## 3. Commitments

Management commits to:

1. run an information security management system aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0;
2. assess information security risks at least quarterly and on significant change, and treat risks above our appetite;
3. set measurable security objectives and review them in management review;
4. give the ISMS the people, budget and tools it needs;
5. meet legal, regulatory and contractual requirements;
6. continually improve the ISMS.

## 4. Principles

- **Least privilege:** people and systems get only the access they need, for as long as they need it.
- **Strong authentication:** multi-factor authentication for all admin, hosting, database and source-control access.
- **Segregation of duties:** nobody approves their own work, reviews their own evidence, or decides on their own access.
- **Secure by default:** security and privacy are designed in; server-side authorisation and row-level security protect data.
- **Evidence over assertion:** controls produce records that can be verified.

## 5. Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Top management | Approves this policy, sets risk appetite, chairs management review |
| ISMS manager (GRC admin) | Runs the ISMS, maintains the hub, reports to management |
| Security lead | Technical security controls, vulnerability and incident management |
| Risk manager | Risk methodology, register and acceptance proposals |
| Information Officer | POPIA compliance, data subject requests, Regulator liaison |
| IT operations | Access provisioning, reviews, backups, endpoint security |
| Architecture | Secure design, architecture and data-flow documentation |
| Control owners | Operate their controls and upload evidence on time |
| Everyone | Follow policies, complete training, report incidents |

## 6. Topic-specific policies

This policy is supported by: Access Control, Acceptable Use, Data Classification, Privacy and Data Protection, Supplier Security, Secure Development, Change Management, Vulnerability Management, Logging and Monitoring, Cryptography, Backup and Recovery, Data Retention, Remote Working, the Incident Response Plan and the Business Continuity and DR Plan.

## 7. Compliance

Compliance is checked through internal audit, access reviews and automated evidence collection. Breaches of policy may lead to disciplinary action under the HR disciplinary process. Exceptions must be recorded in the exceptions register with compensating controls and a review date.

## 8. Review

Reviewed at least annually by the ISMS manager and approved by top management.
