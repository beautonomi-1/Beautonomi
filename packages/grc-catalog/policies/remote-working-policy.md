# Remote Working Policy

## 1. Devices

- Use a company device or an approved personal device meeting the device checklist: full-disk encryption, screen lock at 5 minutes or less, automatic OS updates, endpoint protection, no shared user accounts.
- Keep devices with you or locked away; never leave them unattended in public.

## 2. Networks

- Use trusted networks. On public Wi-Fi, use a personal hotspot or approved VPN.
- Home routers must use WPA2/WPA3 and a changed default admin password.

## 3. Working practices

- Lock your screen whenever you step away (clear screen).
- Don't let others see Restricted or Confidential information; don't print it.
- Take calls about customers or incidents where you can't be overheard.

## 4. Loss or theft

Report immediately to [security@ / #security]. IT operations revokes sessions and, where possible, wipes the device.

Acknowledge this policy in the hub.
