# Privacy and Data Protection Policy

## 1. Purpose

Explain how Beautonomi meets the eight POPIA conditions for lawful processing and, where it applies, the GDPR.

## 2. Accountability (POPIA Condition 1)

- Information Officer: [name], registered with the Information Regulator on [date]. Deputy: [name].
- The Information Officer maintains the compliance framework, the processing register, PIAs and the data subject request log in the hub.

## 3. Processing limitation and lawful basis (Condition 2)

- Every processing activity in the register states its justification (consent, contract, legal obligation, legitimate interest).
- Collect only what is needed. New fields holding personal information need Information Officer sign-off.

## 4. Purpose specification and retention (Conditions 3 and 4)

- Use personal information only for the purposes in the register or compatible purposes.
- Keep it only as long as the Data Retention and Deletion Schedule allows; scheduled jobs delete or anonymise it.

## 5. Information quality (Condition 5)

Users can update their details in the apps. Provider details are verified during onboarding.

## 6. Openness (Condition 6)

Privacy notices in the apps and website describe what we collect, why, who receives it, transfers, retention and rights. The PAIA manual is published at [link].

## 7. Security safeguards (Condition 7)

Safeguards are managed through the ISMS. Operators (processors) must have a written agreement requiring equivalent safeguards (s21). Security compromises are handled under the Incident Response Plan and notified under s22.

## 8. Data subject participation (Condition 8)

- Requests for access, correction, deletion or objection are logged in the hub within 1 working day.
- Verify identity before responding. Respond within [30] days.

## 9. Direct marketing (s69)

Electronic marketing only with opt-in consent, or to existing customers for similar services with an opt-out in every message.

## 10. Cross-border transfers (s72)

Transfers to processors outside South Africa rely on binding agreements providing adequate protection. The vendor register records each vendor's data location.

## 11. High-risk processing

Identity verification, precise location tracking and health/consent information require a privacy impact assessment before launch or significant change.

## 12. GDPR

Where we offer services to people in the EU, we also meet GDPR requirements, including records of processing (Art. 30), processor terms (Art. 28), DPIAs (Art. 35) and 72-hour breach notification (Art. 33).
