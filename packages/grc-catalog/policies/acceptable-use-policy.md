# Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Scope

Everyone with access to Beautonomi systems, data or devices.

## 2. You must

- Use company systems and customer/provider data only for your work.
- Keep devices updated, encrypted and screen-locked (5 minutes or less).
- Use MFA and the approved password manager.
- Report lost devices, suspicious messages or suspected incidents immediately to [security@ / #security channel].
- Return devices and remove local copies of company data when you leave.

## 3. You must not

- Share accounts, passwords or MFA codes.
- Copy customer or provider personal information to personal accounts, personal devices, spreadsheets or AI tools not approved by the security lead.
- Look up customers, providers or colleagues without a work reason.
- Install unapproved browser extensions or software that can read company data.
- Disable security software or bypass controls.
- Use production data for testing.

## 4. Monitoring

Beautonomi logs admin actions and access to systems for security purposes, in line with the Privacy and Data Protection Policy.

## 5. Breaches

Breaches may lead to disciplinary action and, where applicable, legal action.

Acknowledge this policy in the hub.
